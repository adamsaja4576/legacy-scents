Our Story

More Than a Fragrance.

Legacy Scents was created around a simple idea: fragrance is more than something you wear. It is part of how you express yourself, how a moment is remembered, and how you leave an impression.

Designed with an appreciation for understated elegance, Legacy Scents brings fragrance into the rhythm of everyday life. The first release is planned as a 10ml portable body perfume — made to travel with you, from morning to evening.
